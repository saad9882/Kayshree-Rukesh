Our Love Story

Our First Meet

After weeks of a mutual friend trying to get us to meet, Kayshree finally agreed. She’ll probably tell you I was lucky she said yes, because agreeing to meet me meant sacrificing something very important to her — a Formula 1 race!

What neither of us knew was that this ordinary Sunday afternoon was about to change both our lives forever.

“The moment I saw her, everything else seemed to fade away. I remember looking at her and thinking, ‘God, this is her.’ There was just something about her — something I couldn’t quite explain.”

The more we spoke, the more captivated I became by her infectious energy, her laugh, her bubbly personality, and this incredible ability she has to make you feel as though you’ve known her forever.

I also discovered two very important things that day… Firstly, she was the first Indian I had ever met from Nelspruit! I genuinely didn’t know they existed. And secondly, Kayshree has an undeniable talent for convincing people to eat. Before I knew it, she had me trying chicken wings — and I have since learnt that once Kayshree decides you need to try something, resistance is completely futile.

I went into that Sunday thinking I was simply meeting someone for an afternoon. I walked away having met the woman who would become my best friend, my favourite person, my greatest adventure and, ultimately, the love of my life.

She thought she was giving up a Formula 1 race to meet me… Little did we know, that afternoon was the beginning of the greatest journey of our lives — and this is one race I’m incredibly grateful we both won.

Our Journey Together

If there's one thing I've learnt, it's that life is a whole lot more exciting when you're with Kayshree! She's adventurous, full of life, and always dreaming up our next adventure. One minute we're planning a quiet weekend, and the next we're booking flights somewhere across the world. She has this incredible way of making even the simplest moments feel special.

She also has a PhD in shopping! Somehow we both convince each other that "just one more shop" is a brilliant idea... although our bank accounts usually have a very different opinion!

Whether we're dancing around the house, singing songs we only know half the words to, or laughing until our stomachs hurt, she's taught me that happiness isn't found in the destination—it's found in who you're sharing the journey with.

“And if there's one thing I've learnt about Rukesh, it's that God knew exactly what He was doing when He brought him into my life.”

Rukesh has the biggest heart of anyone I've ever met. It’s his quiet strength that makes me feel safe no matter what life throws our way. He is my biggest supporter, my voice of reason when my million ideas take over at 4am in the morning, and the calm to my chaos.

He believes in me even on the days I don't believe in myself, and he has this amazing way of making me laugh when I need it most. He has this incredible ability to love deeply, care wholeheartedly, and make everyone around him feel important. Whether it's family, friends or complete strangers, he always leads with kindness.

He's my best friend, my greatest blessing, my travel partner, my shopping partner, my dance partner, and the person I can't wait to spend forever with.

Together, we've learnt that the greatest adventures aren't measured by the places we've travelled... They're measured by the memories we've created, the laughs we've shared, and the life we're building together.

Our Engagement

If you know Rukesh, you'll know that asking him to keep a secret is like asking me not to shop... it's just not going to happen! So, when he actually managed to keep our engagement a complete surprise, I was genuinely impressed.

It all started in one of my favourite cities in the world... London. We had booked into the beautiful Shangri-La at The Shard, and little did I know that while I thought we were simply enjoying a holiday, Rukesh had been secretly planning one of the most unforgettable days of my life.

His story was that we were rushing off to meet Vikram (his friend). The only problem? He suddenly needed a brand-new shirt... then new sneakers... then he decided he had to shave! At this point, I was thinking, "Since when do you get this dressed up just to meet a friend?"

Then he started telling me what to wear: "Not the black dress. No, not the red one. No, definitely not the white one. Wear the skirt... and heels." I was so confused! My hair wasn't even done, so I was frantically curling it while trying to keep up with him saying, "Come on, we're late!"

“When we arrived at the top floor of The Shard, the doors opened... and suddenly everything made sense. There, overlooking London's breathtaking skyline and the lights of Tower Bridge, was the most beautiful room filled with flowers, candles, a singer performing our special songs, and a photographer capturing every moment. Then the love of my life got down on one knee... and asked me to spend forever with him. Of course, I said YES!”

But Rukesh wasn't finished surprising me. Remember that Formula 1 race I always remind him I missed to meet him on our very first date? Well... he made up for it in the best way possible by taking me to my very first Formula 1 Grand Prix!

As if that wasn't enough, we then celebrated our engagement in the most romantic city in the world—Paris—before finishing our trip at the Wimbledon Men's Finals.

Looking back, it wasn't just an engagement... It was a week filled with love, surprises, laughter, and unforgettable memories. And it reminded me of something I'll never forget: Sometimes, God's greatest surprises are the ones you never see coming.
